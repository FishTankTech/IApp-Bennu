# IApp-Bennu

> bennu (noun) — an ancient Egyptian bird associated with the sun, renewal, and cycles of rebirth.
>
> Like its namesake, Bennu is designed around the idea of recurring patterns, personal history, and renewal over time.

Bennu is a free (iOS/iPadOS) menstrual cycle tracking utility focused on customizable health workflows. Users can log cycles, symptoms, moods, and personal health patterns with a simple interface designed to preserve history and provide meaningful insights without subscriptions or paywalls.

## Status
Work-in-progress.  
Unlike desktop utilities, Bennu requires App Store review and distribution through Apple, so releases may take additional time.

## What it does
- Customizable cycle tracking workflows
- Log periods, symptoms, moods, and health patterns
- Track personal history over time
- View cycle trends and predictions
- Privacy-focused health records (hosted only on your device)
- Expot-able data to provide to health providers
- No subscriptions or paywalls

## Installation // Usage
Bennu will be available through the Apple App Store once aproved 

> It will be available on the iOS (iPhone – primary), and iPadOS (iPad - secondary)
>
> On either, simply download the app, complete the initial setup, and customize your tracking workflow to fit your needs.
